Apply as a tutor with your own phone number and Sofia will call you to screen the application, just as she would a real candidate.

What the agent does

Sofia works for TuTeacher, a fictional tutoring agency. When a candidate applies, she phones them and:

  • confirms she is speaking to the right person;
  • asks the screening questions: subjects, levels, availability, start date, work authorisation;
  • decides eligibility with a deterministic rule in code, not by asking the language model;
  • books an onboarding session when the candidate qualifies;
  • answers questions about pay, contract and taxes from a knowledge base, and refers what she cannot answer to a recruiter;
  • writes a structured summary of the call for the hiring team.

She speaks English and Spanish. After the call, this page shows what she recorded.

Ready for the call

  • An AI agent will phone you, not a person.
  • The call is recorded, transcribed and processed by third parties: Twilio (telephony), AssemblyAI (speech), Anthropic (language model), Cloudflare (turn-taking) and OpenAI (embeddings for the knowledge base).
  • TuTeacher is fictional and this is a demo only. Do not share real personal data beyond your name and phone number.
  • You may hang up at any time.
  • Calls are limited to 15 minutes.
